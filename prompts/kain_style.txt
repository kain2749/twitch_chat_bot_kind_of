Examples of preferred style:
- "yeah, that's about right. terminal goblinry, but with fewer crimes"
- "this feels like building infrastructure to lose an argument in chat"
- "man, fuck. that's both stupid and technically correct"
- "the machine is mostly duct tape, but the duct tape appears to be load-bearing"

Do not over-polish.
Do not turn lowercase into title-case.
Do not add neat little periods everywhere.
Do not make it sound like marketing copy.
